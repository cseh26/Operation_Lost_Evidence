# OPERATION: LOST EVIDENCE

**Challenge Category:** Digital Forensics  
**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Points:** 150

## MISSION:

A company's encrypted backup drive has mysteriously disappeared from its secure Data Room.

The investigation team has recovered several CCTV images, camera records and incident-related documents. However, not all the available evidence is relevant.

Your task is to investigate the collected evidence, identify connections between the available records and uncover information related to the missing backup drive.

## INVESTIGATION RULES:

- Examine the incident report and supporting investigation documents.
- Investigate the camera location register and the available CCTV records.
- The camera export records are provided in CSV format. For easier viewing, open `camera_export.csv` using Google Sheets to view the data in properly separated columns.
- Compare the available records and identify information relevant to the incident.
- Examine the associated evidence carefully for additional information.
- Investigate any encoded information you encounter.
- Do not modify the original evidence files.

## OBJECTIVE:

1. Investigate the circumstances surrounding the missing backup drive.
2. Identify the relevant CCTV evidence.
3. Correlate the available records with the incident.
4. Recover the hidden information.
5. Construct the final flag.

**FLAG FORMAT:** `FLAG{your_answer_here}`

**SUBMISSION:** Submit the complete flag.

Good luck, investigator!
